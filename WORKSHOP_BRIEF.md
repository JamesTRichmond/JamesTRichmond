# Workshop brief

Questions only. The creative direction here is yours, and I would rather build
the thing you actually meant than a good version of something else.

Answer inline — a few words each is plenty, and "don't know yet" is a real
answer that saves me guessing. Anything you skip, I will pick a default for and
record it in `DECISIONS.md` so it is visible rather than silent.

---

## Who the wall is for

1. When you picture someone reading the wall and it going *well* — who is it?
   A hiring manager, another engineer, a potential collaborator, a friend, or
   you six months from now?

2. What do you want them to conclude that they would not conclude from your
   GitHub repo list alone?

3. Is there anyone you would rather it did *not* reach? (Employer, competitor,
   anyone from a previous life.)

## The projects

4. For each of the three — onto, the Prompt → Project chain, AgentiCubed —
   what is the one sentence you would say out loud if someone asked what it is?
   Your words, not marketing ones.

5. Which of the three are you proudest of, and which are you least sure about
   showing? Those are often not the same one, and the gap matters.

6. For each: what is the single moment where someone "gets it"? The screen, the
   interaction, the number — the thing that if they saw only that, they would
   understand.

7. Is there a fourth thing that should be on the wall that I have not named?

8. Anything about these projects that must not appear publicly — real user data,
   a client name, an unannounced idea, an API key in a screenshot?

## The chain

9. Prompt Dashboard → Project Dashboard → what? You said more modules connect
   from there. What are they, even loosely?

10. Is the chain a product you intend to ship, an internal tool for yourself, or
    a thesis you are working out by building it? The tile should be honest about
    which, and I do not know which it is.

## The bench

11. Drive is on the bench. What is your reaction to it — keep, kill, or change?

12. Of the other candidates, which do you want next: the portrait that grades
    differently for day and night, a frame that advances one step every time
    anyone loads the profile, or the engraving in layered parallax planes?

13. Is there something you have wanted the profile to do that I have never
    mentioned? This is the most valuable question here.

## Tone and limits

14. How raw is too raw? "Here is a thing that does not work yet" is fine. Is
    "here is a thing I abandoned because I lost interest" fine?

15. The offcuts section names four approaches that were wrong. Does that read as
    confident or as self-deprecating to you? I think confident, but you are the
    one whose name is on it.

16. Six months from now, what makes this page a success? One sentence.

17. Hard no's — anything I should never put on this page, for any reason.
