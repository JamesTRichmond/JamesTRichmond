How I work

Make it useful.
Make it explainable.

I like work where the interface and the underlying system have to agree. A useful screen needs understandable choices. The code behind it needs to handle the less convenient cases too.

Teaching taught me to pay attention to where an explanation stops working. My studies in information technology, cybersecurity, and digital forensics gave me another way to ask what happened, what the evidence supports, and what still needs checking.

That's also why this site has things you can use. The robot responds to you. The security exercise lets you try the sanitizer. The workshop records ideas while they're still taking shape.
